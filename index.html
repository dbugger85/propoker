ProPoker

Clock, tables and money for poker night

Your tournaments

    ProPoker runs your poker tournament from one phone or tablet: a big blind clock with sounds, players and knockouts, rebuys and add-ons, seating and table balancing on tables drawn from above, chip counts at the breaks, who has paid, the prize pool and payouts, and at the end who sends what to whom.

    • 100% free
    • No ads
    • No tracking
    • No account
    • Works offline

                              Install on iPhone

                              1. Tap the Share button in Safari’s toolbar.
                              2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
                              3. Tap Add. ProPoker is now on your home screen.

                              TV view

                              Opens a second window. Drag it to the TV (set the TV to extend the screen, not mirror it) and make it full screen. It follows everything you do here
